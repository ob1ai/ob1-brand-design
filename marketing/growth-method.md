# OB.1 Growth Method

Every marketing decision starts with the buyer and ends with a number. Channel, ideas and creative sit in between. Any person or agent producing OB.1 marketing runs these steps in order and leaves a record.

## The ten steps

1. **Beachhead.** Pick one narrow segment that shares a problem, shares channels, and would believe each other's stories.
2. **Trigger.** Learn the moment that started the search: what changed, what they used before, what else they considered, what nearly stopped them.
3. **Evidence to ICP.** Build the ideal customer profile from real transcripts and deals. Label every claim as evidence or hypothesis. Quote buyers only in their own words.
4. **Channel.** Let the buyer pick it: where they already pay attention and what they need to see before paying. For OB.1's nameable regional market that means warm referral, personalized email, then rooms where owners gather.
5. **Bottleneck.** Map the funnel (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue) and work only on the step where people fall out.
6. **One metric.** One number per sprint, defined by an event, a time window and a baseline.
7. **Ten ideas.** Ten hooks a day for one brand. Keep the one that completes "someone would share this because ___."
8. **Score.** Impact, confidence and ease (ICE) while data is thin; add reach (RICE) once it can be estimated.
9. **Test.** One audience, one offer, one outcome. Write the bar for a second test before the first one ships.
10. **Teardown.** Pull apart campaigns worth stealing (the hook, the product's role, why people share it, how attention becomes a sale) and reuse the principle on purpose.

## Brand rules that apply to every output

- Follow `DESIGN.md` and the `ob1-write` voice: plain words, short sentences, warm and specific.
- No third-party names without written consent. Describe clients by sector and size.
- No invented numbers. Every figure has a source.
- No failure-rate statistics about AI projects. Frame toward adoption and improvement.
- No em dashes in outward copy.
- Nothing publishes or sends without Chris's approval.
